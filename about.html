About the Council

From Hiroshima,
turning trustworthy AI principles
into verifiable practice.

The Hiroshima AI Assurance Council is an independent, industry-led initiative that develops HAAP through practical proof-of-concept projects. Each PoC tests requirements, verification methods, and assurance records that help organizations formally adopt AI-assisted decisions.

What We Do

PoC by PoC,
HAAP becomes operational.

The Council turns PoC-tested requirements, verification methods, and assurance records into a shared HAAP framework for the formal adoption of AI-assisted decisions.

01 / Test
Run practical PoCs

Apply HAAP to real workflows and document exactly which functions were implemented, tested, and not covered.

02 / Build
Deepen HAAP

Incorporate tested requirements, verification methods, and assurance records into the shared framework.

03 / Publish
Make the basis inspectable

Publish protocol materials, implementation scope, PoC results, and technical evidence for independent inspection.

Independent and Hiroshima-Based

Global principles,
practical implementation.

Hiroshima is internationally associated with the Hiroshima AI Process. The Council operates independently and focuses on a practical question: how to translate trustworthy AI principles into conditions that can be verified before an AI-assisted decision is formally adopted.

01 Independent

The Council does not represent Hiroshima Prefecture, Hiroshima City, the G7, any government body, or the Hiroshima AI Process.

02 No certification claims

The Council does not provide legal certification, regulatory approval, compliance guarantees, or safety endorsements.

03 Technology-neutral participation

Participation does not require exclusive adoption of any single vendor, platform, or technology. The Council is not a sales channel for its representative or technical partner.
